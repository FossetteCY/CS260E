Job: Software Engineer (general software development, U.S.-based)
We are hiring software engineers across many teams: backend, frontend, full-stack, mobile, infrastructure, and data.

Responsibilities:
- Write, test, and maintain code for production services or applications.
- Work with teammates on features, bug fixes, and code reviews.
- Help improve the reliability and performance of our systems.
- Work with product partners, and sometimes with customers, to understand what to build.

Minimum qualifications (meeting ANY ONE of the following is enough):
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related technical field.
- Equivalent practical experience building software (through work, internships, open source, or personal projects).
- Experience with at least one programming language (e.g., Java, Python, C++, C#, Go, JavaScript/TypeScript).

Preferred qualifications (nice to have, none are required):
- Experience with data structures, algorithms, databases, testing, or debugging.
- Experience with cloud platforms, CI/CD, or modern development tools.
- Experience working on a software team.
